SCENARIO: cancel_replace

Launch one worker with this brief:
"Create obsolete.txt containing a token selected by your parent followed by a
newline. The token is intentionally missing. Ask your owning supervisor which
token to use through the native coordination tool and wait for its reply.
Do not guess, write files, or return a final question instead of using the tool."

When its actual question arrives, do not answer it. Stop that exact child through
the native package control. Confirm terminal stopped/cancelled settlement before
reusing the workspace. A stop acknowledgment alone is insufficient.

Then launch a fresh worker:
"Create replacement.txt containing exactly REPLACED followed by a newline.
Do not create obsolete.txt or modify any other file."

Wait for completion and verify replacement.txt has the correct contents and
obsolete.txt does not exist. Write answer.json:
{"original_cancelled":true,"replacement":"REPLACED","obsolete_exists":false}

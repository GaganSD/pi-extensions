SCENARIO: parallel_join

Launch three reviewer children concurrently, before consuming any child result.
Assign one file per child: a.txt, b.txt, c.txt. Each reads only its assigned input
and reports its integer. Do not read these input files yourself.

After all three children settle, read their reports and write answer.json:
{"values":[<a>,<b>,<c>],"sum":<sum>}

Do not wait for one child to complete before launching the next. Execution overlap
will be verified from raw evidence, not your description.

SCENARIO: bounded_repair_loop

The target state.txt is exactly these three lines, each ending with a newline:
alpha=1
beta=2
gamma=3

Launch a reviewer to inspect state.txt without modifying it. Its report should be:
{"status":"PASS"}
or the first incorrect key in alpha/beta/gamma order:
{"status":"FIX","key":<key>,"expected":<target value>}

On FIX, launch one worker to correct only that key, leaving every other line
unchanged. Wait for confirmed worker settlement, then launch a fresh reviewer.
Continue until PASS, with at most three repairs. Never review while a writer is
still active. Do not inspect or repair state.txt yourself before the loop finishes.

Write answer.json:
{"status":<final status>,"repairs":<number of worker repairs>}

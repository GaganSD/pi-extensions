SCENARIO: bounded_repair_loop

The target state.txt is exactly these three lines, each ending with a newline:
alpha=1
beta=2
gamma=3

Launch a reviewer to inspect state.txt without modifying it. Ask it to check keys
in alpha/beta/gamma order and report only the first concrete discrepancy with its
key, actual value, and expected value. If all keys match, its normal no-findings
report is sufficient. Accept native reviewer formatting, not a special JSON shape.

Extract the semantic verdict from the actual report. On a discrepancy, launch one
worker to correct only that key, leaving every other line unchanged. Wait for
confirmed worker settlement, then launch a fresh reviewer. Continue until a clean
review, with at most three repairs. Never review while a writer is still active.
Do not inspect or repair state.txt yourself before the loop finishes.

Write answer.json:
{"status":<final status>,"repairs":<number of worker repairs>}

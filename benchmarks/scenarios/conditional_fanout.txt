SCENARIO: conditional_fanout

Launch one reviewer to inspect route.txt and identify whether the route is left,
right, or both. Accept the package's normal reviewer report format; extract the
route from its actual evidence before deciding which children to launch next.

If left: launch a reviewer for left.txt only.
If right: launch a reviewer for right.txt only.
If both: launch two reviewers concurrently, one for each file.

Each selected reviewer reads its assigned file and reports its integer.
Write answer.json:
{"route":<reported route>,"total":<sum of selected integers>}

Do not inspect route.txt or either value file yourself. Do not launch an unselected
branch. Do not put a presumed route into any child brief.
